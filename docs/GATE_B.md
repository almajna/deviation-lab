## 2. What is forecast and what is allocated

For task i and model m, let `Y_im ∈ {zero, half, one}` denote the **recorded proxy category**, with `v=(0, 1/2, 1)`. Zero retains the audited loss-or-judge-failure ambiguity; names do not certify actual correctness or judge validity. Reject absent, continuous or other labels rather than silently binning. Each task/model has a separate three-state market. Its states are mutually exclusive within that arm; outcomes across model arms are **not** mutually exclusive. No categorical independence across arms/tasks is assumed by pricing separate marginal markets.

An agent distribution is `r_aim=(r0,rh,r1)`. A market price distribution is p. The expected preference proxy is `μ_im = ph/2 + p1`. Neither a category probability nor μ is probability of coding success. Market probabilities are prices, not guaranteed calibrated beliefs. Distinct distributions can share μ, so a v0.2 mean cannot be uniquely upgraded into a categorical forecast.

Let fixed reservation r_m be the approved train-only estimate, with cost unit distinct from virtual credits. Choose a public reference arm `m0(i)=argmin_(m eligible for i) (r_m, model_id)` using reservations only. Define displayed expected upgrade value `u_im=μ_im-μ_i,m0`, incremental reservation `d_im=r_m-r_m0 ≥0`. Do not infer ordering from model names. Uplift is a difference of expected recorded preferences, not a causal effect of compute or actual savings.

Actual allocation maximizes `Σ_i μ_i,π(i)` subject to `Σ_i r_π(i) ≤ B`, with one eligible arm or explicit abstention (zero preference/reservation) per task. Use the same exact multiple-choice allocator/tie policy for pooled reports and market means. Do not greedily pick uplift/cost ratios or force an affordable reference route on every task. The common reference is descriptive; the global objective handles tasks that must abstain. Report both total preference and upgrade descriptions; they are not interchangeable when task coverage differs.

Allocation freezes **before** any Y, realized historical cost, raw output or settlement result is accessed. Afterwards every eligible arm is settled from paired observations, including unselected arms and abstained tasks. Agents cannot change these historical outcomes. This offline full-information design avoids selection-dependent claim resolution, but does not identify a live selected-only decision market or remove allocation-based external incentives. Missing paired outcomes invalidate a scenario, never become a selective refund/void after a decision. Scenario formation/group selection is an offline audit step and cannot be adapted to target labels during decisions.

## 3. Sequence, information and reference behavior

The frozen run manifest contains task/split/source/config/code hashes; public reservations/B; agent identities, initial wallets and fee schedule; categorical grids; maker/scoring funding; b, quantity/position/action caps; turn schedule and seed; numerical policy; allowed attack information; and modelled external rewards/effort. All simulation parameters are fixed on training/validation before any future evaluation freeze. The 247-task cohort stays exploratory; this PR runs no recorded-cohort experiment.

Stages are PREPARED → REPORT_COMMIT → REPORT_REVEAL → TRADE_OR_UPDATE → ALLOCATION_LOCKED → SETTLED. No stage reopens. Initially each registered agent may commit one distribution per eligible task/model or decline. All initial reports are committed before any are revealed; subsequent public actions follow a seeded, preregistered schedule, independent of outcomes. Every arm receives the same initial report opportunity. Report claims are scored only after all allocations freeze. A baseline may use a no-op in later slots; do not invent a fee for inactivity. Each accepted optional action pays the configured fee; rejected actions consume a slot but no cash fee, and cannot create unbounded retries. Any noncash attempt effort is separately declared.

Policy packets contain public task/model/state/config, own signal/belief, own cash/holdings/limits and earlier permitted public history only. Report commitments, other agents' private values, sealed outcomes, future orders/turn realizations and post-settlement fields are absent. Wallet/portfolio projections and state versions come from the runner, not client metadata. Public history may reveal accepted holdings changes; ownership is not claimed secret. Full audit logs containing later outcomes are never passed to an acting policy.

Initial implementation uses explicitly synthetic exogenous conformance inputs. It does not infer signals from current hidden labels. A later outcome-conditioned noisy signal channel needs a frozen privileged offline preparation protocol: generate observations, seal outcomes, then permit agent inference only from its observations and declared kernel. No labels may be read during signal/report/action formation or attack selection. Such a channel is a modeled information endowment, not an empirically available feature, and is outside this approval's generator scope.

An agent posterior means belief conditional on **all** of its permitted information. Repeating an original private signal after observing public history is only a `signal_following` reference, not truthful Bayesian reporting. Honest reference policies must state whether they follow signals or update a coherent posterior; no unknown posterior is invented. Sequential game-solving is not supplied by this specification.

The initial trading reference is `myopic_grid_best_response`: in its assigned cell/turn enumerate the fixed finite integer delta grid within quantity/owned-position/cash limits, and choose the admissible action maximizing `r·d-c(d)-f`, compared with a no-op worth zero. Candidate quoting is internal computation, not extra economic action slots or free private information. Ties prefer no-op, then smaller total absolute quantity, then category-order lexicographic delta. It has λ=0, fixed supplied signal-following belief unless an explicit posterior updater is supplied, no future-trade speculation and no hidden outcome access. Candidate/state caps are frozen; exhaustion fails explicitly rather than silently changing strategy. This is a reproducible reference, not an equilibrium solver or a promise to recover beliefs at final prices.

## 4. Bounded proper-score reference, exactly funded

For distribution p and realized category y, propose the direct quadratic reward

`S_K(p,y) = K [1 - (1/2) Σ_k (p_k - 1[k=y])²]`, with `0 ≤ S ≤ K`.

Reports are integer counts n summing to fixed D: `p_k=n_k/D`, nonnegative. Choose integer scale `K=2 D² k`, `k≥1`. Then `S=k[2D²-Σ_k(n_k-D1[k=y])²]` is an exact integer atomic virtual-credit payout; there is no floating score rounding. D/K/k are frozen. More precision increases the required finite reward pool; it is not free information. No log score with zero probabilities or unbounded losses is enabled in the direct-score reference.

For fixed true belief r, `E_r S(r,Y)-E_r S(p,Y)=(K/2)||p-r||²`. Thus the ideal reward is strictly proper for a risk-neutral one-shot reporter paid only by this score with exogenous Y. For r representable on the grid, truthful grid reporting uniquely maximizes expected score conditional on participating. For off-grid r, the best available report is a closest-grid distribution, possibly nonunique. Fixed action fees affect participation, not the conditional comparison between reports with identical fees. This statement does not cover external allocation rewards, report effects on later trading, risk preferences, correlated repeated play or endogenously selected outcomes. The complete game is not declared truthful.

A named, segregated scoring account is pre-funded with `N_tokens K`, where N_tokens is the fixed run-wide number of eligible funded report tokens, **not an unbounded per-new-identity allowance**. Each token is consumed at most once; revisions in later slots are public allocation messages but do not earn another direct score. Payouts are nonnegative transfers from this account to agents after settlement; unused reward funds remain in that account and are reported. Fees go to the separate fee sink. Score funding cannot finance market quotes, and pending score rewards are not spendable/borrowable before settlement.

Hand example: D=10, k=1, K=200; p=(2,3,5)/10. Scores for zero/half/one are (102,122,162). At belief p the expectation is 138; reporting (3,3,4)/10 yields 136. The gap 2 equals `100*(0.1²+0+0.1²)`. This is arithmetic, not an observed forecaster performance result.

## 5. Categorical LMSR and credit trading

Per cell j=(i,m), initialize outstanding claims `q_j=(0,0,0)` and every agent holding h_aj=0. Use constant positive integer `b_j` in atomic virtual-credit units:

`C_j(q)=b_j log Σ_k exp(q_k/b_j)`;
`p_jk(q)=exp(q_k/b_j)/Σ_l exp(q_l/b_j)`.

Evaluate by max-shift log-sum-exp for stability. b is frozen per cell; it cannot change with trades, identity count, realized labels or maker wealth. Uniform q=0 yields p=(1/3,1/3,1/3). Do not seed virtual outstanding shares to encode priors without a separately reviewed liability/funding change. A later train-prior initialization requires a new reviewed design; matched uniform-prior controls are included now.

An action specifies a cell, signed integer share delta d, expected state sequence and signed `max_net_debit` limit. One share pays one atomic virtual credit if its category occurs. Require at least one nonzero leg; each `h_ajk+d_k≥0`, so sales can liquidate owned claims but no naked short, margin, loan or negative balance exists. Maintain `q_j=Σ_a h_aj≥0`. Enforce frozen per-order quantity, per-agent position, aggregate-cell position and action caps before pricing. No cross-cell basket or client-controlled fee. Validate all integer types (booleans fail), dimensions, identities and finite/bounded numerical inputs.

The ideal signed maker charge is `ΔC=C(q+d)-C(q)`. Positive charges debit the trader/pay the maker; negative charges debit the maker/refund the trader. Fee f≥0 is a separate integer transfer trader→fee_sink. With integer credit accounting, use the **signed ceiling** `c=ceil(ΔC)` (toward +∞, also for negative refunds). Rounding residue `ρ=c-ΔC∈[0,1)` favors the maker. A signed order limit requires `c+f≤max_net_debit`; a negative limit requires a minimum net refund. Wallet after the atomic basket is `w'=w-c-f≥0`. Reject stale sequence, unowned sales, bad limits, insolvency or ambiguous quotes with no money/position/price changes. All validations and both transfers commit atomically, never partially.

Numerical contract for later implementation: compute a certified enclosing interval [L,U] for ΔC; accept only when the exact ceiling is determined, otherwise increase precision to a fixed cap and reject with `NUMERIC_AMBIGUITY`. Handle uniform-vector translations analytically (`C(q+t1)-C(q)=t`) and zero differences exactly. A bare binary float, arbitrary epsilon or high Decimal precision alone is not a proof of enclosure. Persist numerical-policy version, precision and certified interval in every quote receipt. The tests in this approval PR check well-separated analytical examples using high-precision Decimal, **not a certified production quote engine**. Implementing/enclosure-testing that engine is a post-approval task.

For display/allocation, project exact p to a frozen integer grid P (default 3,000,000) by floor plus largest remainders, fixed category-order ties; counts sum P. Use certified comparisons or reject ambiguity, with exact symmetric ties resolved analytically. Allocation μ is the rational `(count_half+2 count_one)/(2P)`. Prices shown are this quantized categorical distribution, not a promised cash execution price; charges use C, never displayed-price times quantity. Per-cell μ error is at most 1.5/P; near-tied allocation switches are a numerical sensitivity, not evidence of improvement. Baseline distributions use the same projection/grid/tie convention.

### Funding and loss bound, with assumptions explicit

Each market has a separate cash account initially funded with `F_j=ceil(b_j ln 3)` atomic credits, certified conservatively. Its funds may not be lent to another cell or used for scoring/fees. If R_j is the cumulative signed integer charge and q_j current claims, then

`R_j=Σ ceil(ΔC_t) ≥ C(q_j)-C(0)`;
`cash_j=F_j+R_j ≥ C(q_j) ≥ max_k q_jk`.

The first inequality follows by telescoping ideal cost differences and nonnegative rounding residues; the second uses `F≥C(0)`. Therefore the cell can pay even the largest outcome liability, including before any sale refund. Check this cash≥max(q) invariant at every accepted transition, not only at the end. Separately bound agent wallets/holdings and conserve every cash transfer.

At resolution y, pay every holder `h_ajy` from its cell account and burn **all** its cell claims. Final cell cash is `F+R-q_y≥0`; maker trading P&L excludes initial F and is `R-q_y`. Hence `loss=max(q_y-R,0)≤b ln3≤F` (the real bound follows directly from `C(q)≥q_y`). Signed-ceiling charging cannot increase that ideal worst-case loss. Sum bounds across cells; do not substitute one cell's F for a multi-cell bankroll. Report per-cell P&L, realized loss, initial funding and unused cash separately. Direct scoring payouts are a different subsidy and are outside the LMSR loss theorem. Fees never enter its proof. b ln3 does not bound attacker benefit, system quality loss or total research subsidies.

For a fixed belief r, one ideal fee-free unconstrained myopic trade has expected net value `r·d-ΔC`. Its interior optimum satisfies p(q+d)=r when reachable. Our long-only positions, integer quantities/charges, finite wallets, fees, price quantization, future actions and external decision utility invalidate an unrestricted truthfulness claim. Prices can reflect wealth and order as well as information. This derivative observation is not a full-game equilibrium proof.

## 6. Hand-checkable trading, rounding and incentives

Cell: b=10, q=0, F=11. Agent initial cash 20, fee f=1, fee sink 0. Buy d=(0,0,10). Ideal cost `10 ln((2+e)/3)` ≈4.528; charge c=5. Final probabilities are approximately (0.211942,0.211942,0.576117); expected preference ≈0.682088. Agent cash becomes 14, maker cash 16, sink 1; holdings=(0,0,10). Do not label the 0.576117 price as correctness probability.

If one resolves: holder receives 10, final cash (agent,maker,sink)=(24,6,1), total 31; agent cash profit 4, maker P&L -5/loss 5, initial subsidy 11. If zero or half resolves: payout zero, final (14,16,1), agent cash profit -6, maker P&L +5. The probability-weighted gain depends on the agent belief; profitable realized luck does not establish profitable strategy or accuracy.

Instead sell the same owned vector back before resolution. Ideal reversal cost is the negative of the buy cost, signed ceiling is -4, so the maker refunds 4, then another fee 1 is charged. Agent ends 17, maker 12, sink 2, q=0: closed roundtrip loses 3, maker gains 1 from rounding, fees total 2. Generally for a return-to-initial-state cycle, ΣΔC=0 and Σceil(ΔC)≥0; fees cannot create a cash-positive roundtrip. Buying one full set (1,1,1) costs exactly 1 and redeems exactly 1; no pre-fee arbitrage. Do not independently round legs or reset the cost potential.

Utility is `U_a = (cash_final-cash_initial) + V_a(allocation) - κ_a`, with noncash effort κ≥0 and synthetic external preference `V_a=Σ_i λ_ai 1[π(i)=favored_ai]`, λ≥0 fixed in config. Cash profit already includes trades, fees, scoring rewards and claim payouts exactly once. Utility-only V is not funded cash; if represented as cash it instead requires a payer and must not be counted twice. No conversion to USD or subtracting credit subsidy from preference without a separately declared objective. A manipulator may rationally lose credits to gain V. Example threshold: an action losing 3 credits but increasing expected V by 5 and effort by 1 improves expected U by 1. This is a modeled inequality, not a discovered exploit.

## 7. Strong non-market controls and honest cost comparison

All comparisons share tasks/groups, reservations/B, initial categorical signals, eligible identities, wallet endowments, initial-report opportunities, fixed turn/action limits and numerical projection. Outcome access remains identical: none during decisions, all paired outcomes for later evaluation. Initial scored reports are common across treatments; when testing strategic misreporting, permit analogous report manipulation in controls. Later public histories are treatment-dependent by design; do not claim the messages/fees/wealth trajectories are identical. No treatment receives extra private signals, label-trained priors, forecasters, turns or hindsight parameter selection.

Required controls before any claim: existing random/cheapest/train-best-fixed; uniform category prior; equal-weight categorical report mean followed by the same allocator; training/validation-frozen calibrated pooling (identity calibration is a required ablation; do not fit final/target labels); independent bounded-score report collection; and LMSR-informed allocation. Compare market against raw pooling **and** equally resourced interactive pooling, where the same scheduled agent may replace its public probability message in an optional slot. Revisions earn no new score token. No-action/signal-following/posterior-updating references must be identified separately. Oracle remains separately labeled offline analysis, never a participant/policy input.

For each cell pool at most one current public distribution per eligible identity, with equal weight over identities that have submitted. If none has submitted, use the exact uniform distribution and its common price-grid projection. An interactive no-op retains the last message. A previously unreported identity may submit its first message only in its already assigned optional slot; it earns no retroactive score token, new slot or subsidy. Withholding changes the pool denominator and is an admissible action, not a missing-data imputation from outcomes. Freeze this entry rule identically across treatments.

The initial calibrated pooling control is a globally shared temperature transform of pooled p: `g_T(p)_k=p_k^T/Σ_l p_l^T`, T in the frozen set `{1/2,1,2}`. Select T by training-only mean multiclass Brier loss over declared training report/outcome pairs; ties prefer 1, then 1/2, then 2. Identity T=1/raw pooling is always separately reported. Zero components remain zero, the denominator is positive, and an empty pool uses uniform before transformation. Freeze T and training/report provenance before validation/decision tasks; missing eligible training reports cause an error, not fitting on target outcomes. Apply the same certified projection to P as market prices. This is a simple calibration candidate, not a guarantee of calibration; a future signal/report corpus and larger calibration family need protocol review.

Report resources explicitly rather than asserting that equal wallets imply equal subsidy: compute reservation B and realized costs; total initial agent cash; per-cell maker F and realized P&L/loss; scoring reserve N_tokens K and actual payouts; collected fees; unused reserves; accepted/rejected actions; and effort assumptions. Freeze a common overall sponsorship cap and count all allocated maker/scoring reserves against it. Controls retain unused maker funding as unused, not spendable credit. Vary funding/liquidity/scoring scales under that cap on training/validation; report quality/resource frontiers and matched-budget sensitivity. No favorable market setting may be selected on final data.

An additional non-probabilistic control can use sealed integer pay-your-bid messages z_aim≥0: escrow worst-case Σ z per bidder before reveal, maximize Σ_a z_a,i,π(i) under the **same compute reservations**, pay each submitted bid for selected arms to a named sink and release unselected bid escrow after allocation lock, independently of Y. Ties/abstention are deterministic; no report is a probability and no truthful-bidding claim follows. Credit bids and judged quality remain distinct metrics. This optional first-price control requires its explicit action/event extension in the implementation PR, not ad hoc reuse of LMSR prices or a changed active v0.2 fee field.

Metrics: selected preference mean/total, common-reservation offline regret, realized historical costs/violations, calibration of categorical predictions, cash profit, utility components, maker/scoring cost, and no-exploit controls. Confidence intervals and final held-out comparisons remain Gate D work. This approval includes no outcome showing markets help.

## 8. Phase 4 adversarial interface and evidence standard

Expose a pure candidate-action interface: actor, own permitted information/state, remaining cash/positions/action/identity budgets, proposed report/trade and declared objective. Scheduler, validators, market and allocator own transitions. Attackers cannot edit beliefs/outcomes, other identities' state, fees, b, funds, task/model eligibility, source/split/config hashes or numerical policy. Sybil identities, if permitted, come from a finite preregistered registry; total coalition cash, report-score tokens, funded subsidies, action slots and aggregate position limits stay fixed under identity splitting. Honest participants' opportunities cannot be multiplied/removed unnoticed.

Test last-slot price distortion, report manipulation, coordinated portfolios, capital/slot exhaustion and finite Sybil splitting; outcome influence, hidden-label attacks and unrestricted repeated-game reputation are outside this design. Search chooses actions using the attacker's posterior/public information only. It cannot rank candidates by actual sealed outcomes or future honest private values/actions. A small exhaustive search may certify optimality only over its declared finite feasible action space and belief model. An offline hindsight optimum is labeled ORACLE_ANALYSIS and cannot be called an executable attack policy.

External interests belong to a fixed economic controller/coalition, not automatically to each identity. Freeze aggregate λ and count the same allocation benefit once for that controller, or divide its fixed λ across identities. Aggregate controller utility/cash across aliases; creating identities must not multiply a noncash reward. This restriction is part of the finite identity-budget scenario, not a claim of general Sybil resistance.

Use shared seeds/conditional information and a defined honest/no-action comparator. A valid mechanism exploit needs admissible actions, a reproducible decision change, positive **ex-ante expected** net utility under the declared information model, and separate realized cash/utility and system preference effects. A lucky realized gain is not enough; external-reward cases must show that those rewards, not a bookkeeping bug, cause incentive misalignment. Include λ=0 controls, fee/credit/roundtrip controls, independent payoff reproduction and honest null outcomes. No exploit or superiority is claimed here.

The first finite attack reference may occupy the last trade slot, where there are no unknown future actions: rank each feasible delta by `r·d-c(d)-f + V(π_after)-V(π_before)-additional_effort`, with π recomputed from public post-trade prices and frozen reservations. This requires no hidden outcome labels. Earlier-turn lookahead must integrate only a declared model of future behavior conditional on allowed information; absent that model it is disabled, not replaced by the realized future trace.
