Before fitting or checking any efficacy metric, a custodian must download only the three locked members and verify byte lengths and hashes, confirm model snapshot strings and one judgment per arm, publish overlap/exclusion/coverage counts, and construct an outcome-free prompt projection. Exclude ambiguous aliases and duplicates under the frozen rules.
