# Interactive website tournament

The existing visual-lab branch and its untracked prototypes were inspected without modification. Its categorical simplex supplied a useful geometry and recorded-game model, but its timeline-driven interaction did not meet the new brief. Five independent working prototypes were then built in isolated directories. The two finalists were refined separately.

## Refinement and selection

The landscape enumerated 27 assignments. Independent checks compared every allocation, cost and objective over 1,010 configured budget/report combinations with the repository's Python optimizer for that scenario. It lacks participant transfers, strategic utility and an authentic recorded-experiment connection.

The mechanism finalist was rebuilt around the actual categorical game: 66 integer reports plus decline, quadratic scoring, allocation threshold and external reward. Its standalone model reproduced the 335 available landscape entries in the prior redesign's recorded fixture and the six recorded conditional optima; a simulated DOM-handler smoke tested controls. Separately, a reviewer reproduced all 402 choices across six cases against the Python engine. These tests do not certify arbitrary new scenarios.

The mechanism lab was selected for integration because it provides an immediately understandable strategic choice, explicitly accounts for expected incentives, and connects directly to recorded economic decisions. The landscape's simultaneous comparison of outcomes and explanation informed the integrated interaction. The preserved recorded replay remains separate. The independent source assessment is not an external user study or proof of visual quality.

## Boundaries

Interactive edits are hypothetical, computed in the browser and denominated in virtual credits; they are not new LLM calls, deployment forecasts, or independently verified certificates. The recorded certificate is conditional on the later-observed rival report and the stated belief. The model did not see the rival's realized report at its decision time. LiteLLM remains INCONCLUSIVE. No frozen protocol, research evidence or final holdout is modified.
