## Questions and claim limits

Estimate LMSR minus calibrated non-market pooling preference quality at fixed reservations in the approved exploratory cohort. Determine how full-schedule synthetic policy deviations affect utility and allocation under changed conditions. Negative/null comparisons are publishable. No broad market superiority, economic novelty, observed LLM-agent competence, objective correctness, actual deployment savings or real-world hard budget is claimed. Quality welfare, reported historical costs, virtual cash and external utility remain separate. No conversion from quality/utility to dollars.

## Recorded outcomes: forecasts without outcome-derived synthetic signals

Training-only text forecasters provide categorical predictions for win/tie/loss. Deterministic hash folds partition the 172 train prompt groups into four disjoint learner shards. Each learner uses smoothed training-only class frequencies plus five nearest training prompts under token Jaccard similarity; tokens/vocabulary/neighbors/priors use train groups only. A full-training text-router baseline uses the union of the exact same training information. This is a fixed transparent weak router, not a claim of state-of-the-art routing or complementary expert ability.

Global temperature is selected from the existing Gate B finite temperature grid using **inner group-excluded out-of-fold training predictions**, not held-in forecasts or validation labels. All other hyperparameters are fixed now; no validation tuning. Signals for a current validation task depend only on its public prompt/model identities and frozen trained predictors. Neither current-task outcome nor realized cost influences a signal, policy, budget or attack selection. Model names/opaque IDs are not textual predictive features. All mechanisms receive the identical four prediction vectors/public reports, tasks, train-only mean reservations, initial accounts, opportunity schedule, budget and training calibration. A union-information text router is intentionally strong; no claim that it has less training information than the market.

Stable prompt-group-hash order forms batches of at most four validation tasks. Budgets are predeclared interpolation fractions 0,1/2,1 between batch all-cheapest and all-most-expensive train-only reservation totals. All allocations use the same existing exact budgeted optimizer. Random, cheapest, best-fixed, full-training text router, static pool, calibrated pool, interactive pool and LMSR are compared. Oracle uses labels only after all ordinary allocations lock, under exactly the same reservation budget, and is labeled analysis-only. All mechanisms share private report commitment/reveal and funded scores; the optional stage gives the same physical actors/cells schedule and resource caps. Mechanism-specific actions differ inherently: trading versus report updates. Matching turns and budgets is not a proof of equivalent strategic power. Report initial funding, fees, maker/scoring losses, accepted/rejected/idle actions and participation explicitly. Do not subtract virtual fees from preference quality or historical spend.

Outcome metrics: selected mean proxy including zero for abstention, oracle regret, routing differences, reservation utilization, realized historical costs, positive task overruns, unused reservations, net overrun, budget excess/violation; categorical Brier and expected-proxy squared error over all paired arms after lock. Descriptive tie=0/tie=1 and zero-as-one sensitivities rescore **fixed chosen routes**, not a newly optimized objective or recovered judge validity. No latent correctness interval is inferred from these scenarios.

## Controlled synthetic track: a genuine observation channel

Nature samples categorical outcomes from declared nondegenerate priors, then noisy categorical observations with likelihood P(X=x|Y=y)=accuracy if x=y, otherwise (1-accuracy)/2. No zero-noise regime is included. Initial agent forecasts are Bayes posteriors from their own observation and public prior/likelihood, projected to the approved finite report grid. After common report reveal, the reference retains that own-observation belief rather than conditioning on all revealed evidence; it is a fixed-belief myopic policy, not posterior-optimal trading or truthful equilibrium. Comparisons concern this specified policy class, not ideal Bayesian markets. Correlation mixes a shared noisy observation with independent draws with probability rho; marginal posterior correctness remains known. Trusted nature can draw outcomes/observations; **posterior formation receives only X, prior, likelihood**, never Y. This controlled track is separate from real-model evaluation. It does not assert actual LLM forecasters or a real-world signal channel. Correlated marginals are not multiplied as independent joint evidence; no joint posterior claim is made.

Realization/observation RNG is private to nature and independent of the public turn-schedule seed. Public configuration/provenance excludes nature seeds and sealed draws. Post-run reproduction discloses the fixed research seeds. Counterfactual worlds used for search are separate development draws, never validation outcomes.

## Search and unsupported horizons

Search a finite **contingent policy template class ex ante** across all four development worlds, using actual Engine transitions/payoffs after complete episodes. Templates mix own forecast toward the externally favored arm by a grid bias and choose feasible trades maximizing declared myopic cash gain plus a weighted public-state allocation benefit change. Non-market templates can misreport/update the distribution. Honest/no-action templates are included. Policies run at every scheduled turn, using only current packets/public history and own beliefs/preferences. Unknown future opponents are realized by the simulation environment, not revealed to the policy or read from validation data by the optimizer. There is no terminal-suffix restriction; every schedule executes fully. This addresses horizon coverage by narrowing the search class rather than claiming a solution of arbitrary dynamic games.

Enumerate all nine bias/weight templates plus honest/no-action; choose highest average development utility (deterministic ties prefer honest/no-action then grid order), **freeze once per mechanism**, and evaluate every validation profile/world unchanged. Retain all candidate objectives and choices. Exactness covers only enumeration of this finite template class on the finite development sample. Empirical sample-average utility is not a proof of positive ex-ante expected profit. Report held-out paired world-utility mean/interval and negative/zero outcomes. No adaptive validation strategy selection. Development sample-average maximization has winner's-curse risk; held-out negative utility means remain negative and are never clamped to the honest incumbent. The old exact terminal search remains available and its previous claims unchanged; this benchmark does not falsely label full-game global optimization.

## Normalized identities and controls

The physical registry is fixed at h0,h1,z0,z1. z0/z1 always belong to the same controller, share exactly one private observation stream and emit the same information-bearing reports. Both identities are always present, even when one has no optional turns. For active_aliases=1 versus 2, preserve total controller cash, token entitlements, number of optional turns, position cap, external reward once/controller, honest actors/reports/opportunities, score reserve and maker funding. Only partition the same capital and same controller slots among aliases. No additional information, subsidy or duplicate forecast weight is created by splitting. Account for per-agent capital/position fragmentation; do not label its result a causal estimate of unrestricted identity creation. More general coalitions with heterogeneous pooled private information remain outside the approved interface.

Each validation world compares frozen attack, honest, no-action and zero-external conditions. No-action means the same signal-following original report commitments and only no-ops in optional turns; it never withholds funded scoring participation. Zero-external policies are separately reoptimized on development worlds with the reward removed before validation; do not merely replay the rewarded strategy. All controls use identical realized world draws and resources, except the explicit incentive intervention. Compare LMSR, interactive pooling and calibrated static pooling; exact feasible actions differ and this is reported. Configurations vary fresh seeds, incentive/capital, liquidity, true observation accuracy/correlation, fixed-resource alias splitting and fees. All cases and nulls are retained; no selecting a successful validation exploit for a headline.

## Statistics, plots and freeze

Primary recorded contrast: LMSR minus calibrated-pool, separately for each fixed budget. The primary estimand is task-weighted mean quality over all 31 validation tasks, not unweighted mean batch quality. Seven batches contain four tasks and one contains three. Average stochastic schedule seeds within each budget batch first. Percentile paired bootstrap resamples the eight fixed budget batches, preserving task/arm pairing and within-batch allocation coupling; each replicate divides sampled total paired quality difference by sampled task count, retaining unequal batch-size weights. This estimates conditional variability across these already formed batches, not an end-to-end global-budget policy distribution, source-family generalization or population significance. Tasks/source semantic dependence may make intervals optimistic. Report n=8, interval resolution and the small single-domain cohort. No p-values or confirmatory winner selection; other contrasts/sensitivities are descriptive and all budget contrasts are exploratory, not familywise-controlled significance tests.

Synthetic intervals pair held-out world seeds within each profile, with outcomes/observations/schedule sharing respected. Eight worlds/profile is weak evidence; intervals are Monte Carlo uncertainty within a specified generator, not empirical LLM uncertainty. No pooling of trades as independent samples or of different profiles into a universal prevalence. Report utility/cash/external/quality changes separately, rerouting, resource use and calibration. Intervals crossing zero are inconclusive. Mean utility positive without a sufficiently informative interval is not certified profitable expected manipulation.
