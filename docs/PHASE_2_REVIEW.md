## Limits

This engine uses trusted in-process policies; it is not hostile-code isolation. Data/source authenticity remains a caller responsibility. Cost records parsed as floats by Phase 1 cannot regain source decimal precision; exact fitting uses their declared decimal strings. Sparse exact allocation fails explicitly at its configured state cap. Fixed illustrative transfers are accounting inputs, not a proper scoring rule or market outcome. Exogenous synthetic signals are not observed autonomous-agent behavior.

No recorded-cohort reservation fit, empirical comparison, attack, market, frontend, final holdout or deployment was run. No superiority, verified billing, objective coding correctness, actual savings or real-world budget guarantee is established. Later Gate B and evaluation/release approvals remain necessary. $0; no automatic merge.
