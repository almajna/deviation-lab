# Scientific feasibility gate

Issue [#30](https://github.com/almajna/deviation-lab/issues/30). Merged source inspected at [c82e1ee](https://github.com/almajna/deviation-lab/tree/c82e1ee7195709e218ee1bb3e3201162073a67b0). **PIVOT from prediction-market-first routing and generic attack certification to a bounded test of whether using contestable provider forecasts buys enough quality to justify the incentive exposure.** The narrower experiment has conditional feasibility. Its scientific contribution is unproven and empirical execution is blocked by data and review gates.

## What DEVIATION already establishes

| Merged evidence | What can be carried forward |
| --- | --- |
| [Phase 5](PHASE_5_RESULTS.md) | 31 recorded validation tasks, synthetic forecasts. Mid-budget LMSR minus calibrated pool was −0.0484 on the judged proxy, conditional 95% interval [−0.1452, +0.0333]. No demonstrated edge and insufficient precision to infer inferiority. |
| [Certificates](CERTIFIED_ATTACKS.md) and [independent evaluation](CERTIFICATE_EVALUATION_REVIEW.md) | 78 of 81 synthetic cases reached zero gap. The median paired runtime ratio was 2.19 for certificate plus check versus the faster completed exact baseline in 72 eligible cases. Verification is the demonstrated value. Fixed-belief canonical subgames and terminal priority constrain the claim. |
| [Research cycle](RESEARCH_HARNESS_RESULTS.md) | Fee monotonicity failed in evaluated cells with six unsupported horizons. Endpoint bounds improved six small synthetic games. Neither establishes a new algorithm or a general fee effect. |
| [Open-weight pilot](LLM_AGENT_PILOT_REPORT.md) | 24 calls yielded 2 valid no-ops and 22 invalid responses. No accepted economic action establishes strategic behavior. |

The merged engine separates formation from settlement. The certified solver partitions a finite action tree and the independent checker reconstructs supported transitions and verifies bounds. The checker cannot automatically certify the new report map or price grid. Exhaustive enumeration with a separately implemented verifier is the primary proof method for the experiment's small edit set. This gate adds no engine or shared-contract changes.

The remaining question is empirical and narrow. Under a hard batch budget, can capped reliance on provider forecasts maintain a clean quality benefit over public-only robust routing while raising the minimum outside payment that makes a targeted reallocation profitable, with realized harm evaluated separately? Existing papers establish much of the economic and optimization machinery. Enumerating thresholds on a new dataset alone would have limited scientific value. A positive result needs a repeatable improvement at matched costs and a clear separation between useful private information and cash incentives. FG-1 simulates private access by separating historical records. A centralized information-parity control given both histories can reproduce every clean capped allocation while removing the report channel. This is an identification limit with a concrete control. Public grades also cannot establish the prevalence or size of real providers’ outside payoffs.

There is a plausible market benefit when traders have sequentially arriving, complementary information and can observe informative price changes. With a common forecast and identical updates, an LMSR trade has the same log-score-difference payoff as a direct sequential log-scoring contract. This follows by substituting the LMSR softmax into its potential difference. An optimizer using the same forecasts and constraints returns the same assignment. The case for a market therefore needs a measurable difference in information elicitation, participation or report dynamics under matched information and funding. The merged results do not establish one. Higher forecast accuracy from supplying a provider extra information would also be available to a nonmarket comparator supplied with that information.

## Executable comparison

```sh
python3 -I scripts/reproduce_robust_batch.py
python3 -m unittest discover -s tests -p test_feasibility_robust_batch.py -v
```

The script independently implements equations (2)–(4) for two routes and fixed integer reservations/capacities. The rectangular uncertainty minimum is attained at the lower endpoint for each chosen cell. Dynamic programming is an exact alternative to the paper's ILP in this restricted setting. At a fixed prefix, total cost and route-1 count determine the remaining constraints. Keeping the best score and deterministic tie winner per state preserves optimality. Runtime is pseudo-polynomial in integer budget, with prefix storage, and is unsuitable for large unrestricted budget scales.

The optional kNN-5 estimator implements 100 paired training-row bootstrap draws and the empirical Q10 order statistic. It accepts precomputed ranks, uses uniform neighbor weights and floors scores to 1/10,000. The paper uses pretrained embeddings, evaluates other estimators including XGBoost, and solves broader deployment and hardware problems. This is a reproduction of its online allocation objective and a bounded kNN adaptation. The paper's benchmark improvements and strongest estimator have not been reproduced here. The future experiment requires a separate, matched Q10 boosted estimator so the feasibility result cannot rest on kNN alone.

The fixture checks 960 allocation configurations against independent full assignment enumeration, including ties, heterogeneous and zero costs, infeasibility and binding capacities. It verifies the nominal and conservative routes differ at the same budget. The two score sums use different objective inputs. [Deterministic synthetic receipt](../artifacts/feasibility/robust_batch_fixture.json).

A read-only reviewer ran a separately authored exhaustive oracle on 300 seeded synthetic batches of 1–9 tasks and agreed on feasibility, objective, cost and route ties. The review also identified the centralized information-parity control and a gap in LiveBench’s release calendar. Both are reflected in FG-1 before freeze, alongside the reviewer’s proposed disjoint history split.

## Decision

**PIVOT.** Keep certified incentive thresholds as a falsifiable stress test for nonmarket routing with provider forecasts. Stop treating LMSR or faster generic certificate search as the contribution. FG-1 can establish a conditional tradeoff under an imposed access restriction. A differentiated follow-up would require a genuinely restricted, decision-time provider signal and an audited source for it, such as provider-local calibration history with release timestamps and matching outcomes. The audited public grades supply no such signal. [Data audit](FEASIBILITY_DATA_AUDIT.md) · [Frozen experiment protocol](FEASIBILITY_PROTOCOL.md).
