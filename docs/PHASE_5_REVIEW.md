## Limits of independent evidence

Shared model family and common engine dependencies can share mistakes. Exact enumeration proves only finite sample/class selection; no theoretical expected-profit or global-optimum proof exists. Weak fixed-belief forecasters, tiny single-domain selected real sample, eight Monte Carlo worlds/profile, fixed schedule, intrinsic action differences, duplicate controlled report weights and conditional bootstrap uncertainty limit claims. The loader/CLI trusts pinned repository manifests and review receipts; it is not a hostile-input authorization boundary. No final holdout, public research superiority or release is approved. Human exact-head review and merge remain separate.
